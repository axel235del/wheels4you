Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wheels 4 You ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our website or contact us to enquire about or book a self-drive rental car in Indore.

Information We Collect

When you use the booking form on our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any message or booking details you choose to share (such as preferred car, dates, or pickup/delivery preference)

If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, your phone number and the contents of your message are received through WhatsApp directly, in line with WhatsApp's own privacy practices.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share with us only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or booking request
  • Confirm car availability, pricing, and rental details with you
  • Get in touch regarding your reservation, pickup, or return

We do not use your information for marketing without your consent, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Sharing of Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except where necessary to operate our booking process (for example, communicating with you via WhatsApp) or where required by applicable law.

Data Retention

We aim to retain enquiry and booking information only for as long as reasonably needed to manage your booking and for basic record-keeping. If you would like us to delete information you've shared with us, you can reach out using the contact details below and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.

Cookies & Tracking

At present, this website does not use cookies or third-party analytics tracking. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect that.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms such as Instagram or WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

We try to handle your information responsibly, but as a small local business, our processes may be informal. If you have any concerns about your data, please reach out to us directly and we'll do our best to help.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page.
